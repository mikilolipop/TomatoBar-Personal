# 眨眼素材生成

内置 ImageGen；编辑参考为 assets/tomy-selected-master.png；transparent_background=true。
生成后保存为 assets/tomy-blink-strip.png。实际输出为 2172×724，3 个等宽格。
播放器不切换整张角色，只引用眼部区域。以下是实际提示词。

Use the attached approved tomato mascot as an EXACT identity-preserving edit target.
Output one transparent 3-FRAME BLINK STRIP: exactly 3 equal square cells laid out horizontally, no gaps and no outer border. Target 1536x512 pixels; each cell 512x512. Duplicate the same approved mascot three times. Each copy has identical silhouette, pixel color clusters, five-leaf crown (copy exactly from source), face position, eye spacing, arms, feet, body proportions, illumination, highlight and smile. Character center x=256 in each cell, feet baseline y=460; character occupies 360x390 at most, whole body complete.
Cell A: original open-eyed pose.
Cell B: SAME original pose, both eyes half closed. Only change the dark eye pixels within their original bounding rectangles.
Cell C: SAME original pose, both eyes fully closed into short dark curved eyelid lines. Only change eye pixels within their original bounding rectangles.
This is duplication plus eyelid edits, not three redrawings. Do not change pose, leaf shapes, body width, head angle, cheek positions, outline or palette between cells. No inhaling, tilting, looking sideways, waving, mirroring or turning. Keep every pixel of the body outside the eye regions as stable as possible. The open-eyed first cell must faithfully match the exact selected rounded standing tomato, never squashed, pear-shaped, or soft-clay restyled.
Maintain the source's crisp pixel-art stepped edges, warm saturated coral red, dark red volume shadows and muted green crown. No furniture or accessories. No text, labels, grid lines, interface, frame numbers, backdrop, mockup, watermark or rendered checkerboard. Real transparent alpha around all three characters. Flat orthographic asset strip, natural 3:1 canvas.
